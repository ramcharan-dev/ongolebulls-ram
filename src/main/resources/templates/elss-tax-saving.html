What is ELSS?

Equity Linked Saving Scheme (ELSS) is a tax-saving mutual fund that combines tax benefits with wealth creation potential

Equity Linked Saving Scheme

ELSS is a diversified equity mutual fund that invests primarily in stocks of companies across market capitalizations. It offers the dual benefit of tax savings under Section 80C of the Income Tax Act along with the potential for higher returns through equity investments.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about ELSS and tax-saving investments.

  • ELSS (Equity Linked Savings Scheme) is a tax-saving mutual fund that invests primarily in equities. It offers deduction under Section 80C up to ₹1.5 lakh and has a 3-year lock-in period.
  • ELSS has a mandatory lock-in of 3 years from the date of each investment. This is the shortest among 80C options like PPF (15 years) and tax-saving FDs (5 years).
  • You can save up to ₹46,800 in taxes (at 30% slab) by investing ₹1.5 lakh in ELSS under Section 80C. The exact benefit depends on your income tax slab.
  • Yes. You can invest in ELSS through SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) or lumpsum. SIP helps you invest regularly and spread your tax saving across the year.
  • ELSS offers potential for higher returns as it invests in equity, with only a 3-year lock-in. PPF and FDs are debt-based with longer lock-ins. Choice depends on your risk appetite and goals.
